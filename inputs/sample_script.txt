The best telescopes launched in 2024.
Webb shocked us again this year.

The James Webb Space Telescope delivered its most detailed image yet of a galaxy cluster 4.6 billion light-years away, revealing thousands of individual stars for the first time.

Scientists confirmed that Webb's NIRCam instrument captured light from some of the earliest galaxies ever observed, potentially dating back to just 400 million years after the Big Bang.

Meanwhile, the Euclid space telescope released its first full-color deep field images, mapping dark matter distribution across 10 million galaxies.

These discoveries are reshaping our understanding of cosmic evolution — and we're only getting started.
